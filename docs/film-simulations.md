# Fuji Film Simulation Reference

Use these characteristics as a guide when mapping Fuji looks to BIONZ X ISP parameters.

| Film Simulation | Contrast | Saturation | Key Color & Tonal Characteristics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Provia (Standard) | Medium | Medium | Faithful rendering; the standard all-rounder. |
| Velvia (Vivid) | Very High | Very High | Deep shadows; ultra-vivid reds and greens for landscapes. |
| Astia (Soft) | Soft | Medium-High | Smooth skin tones; vibrant but natural highlights. |
| Classic Chrome | High | Low | Cyan-shifted blues; muted colors for a documentary look. |
| Classic Negative | High | High | Based on Superia; faded shadows and warm highlights. |
| Nostalgic Neg. | Soft | Medium-High | 1970s look; amber highlights and high shadow detail. |
| Reala Ace | Hard | Medium | High-fidelity color reproduction with distinct, hard tonality. |
| Eterna (Cinema) | Very Soft | Very Low | High dynamic range; desaturated for cinematic grading. |
| PRO Neg. Hi | High | Medium | Contrast-heavy; good for portraits in flat lighting. |
| PRO Neg. Std | Very Soft | Medium-Low | Neutral tones; best for controlled studio environments. |
| Acros (Standard) | High | N/A | Dynamic B&W; ISO-dependent grain and rich shadows. |
| Acros + Ye/R/G | High to Ultra | N/A | Digital filters to darken skies (Ye/R) or soften skin (G). |
| Monochrome (Std) | Medium | N/A | Flat, neutral B&W conversion; simple and clean. |
| Monochrome + Ye/R/G | Medium-High | N/A | Standard B&W with classic contrast-adjusting filters. |
| Sepia | Medium-Soft | N/A | Warm, reddish-brown tint for an aged, archival look. |
